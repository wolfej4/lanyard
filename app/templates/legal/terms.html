  • If the organizer cancels the event on {{ S }}, every paid ticket is refunded in full to the original payment method automatically.

Refunds usually reach your account within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Please contact the organizer before disputing a charge with your bank. Filing chargebacks in bad faith, for example after attending, can get your account suspended.
