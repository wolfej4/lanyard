1. Who we are

{{ O }} ("we", "us") runs {{ S }} at {{ settings.base_url }} and is responsible for the personal information described here.

When you register for an event, the organizer receives some of your information (see section 4) and becomes responsible for how they use it. Our Terms of Service only allow organizers to use it to run their event, but their own handling of it isn't covered by this policy.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, and the name you choose to display.
  • Registrations: which events you register for, the gamer tag you enter, your ticket type and price, your ticket code, your seat, your poll votes, your tournament signups, and any note you send an organizer, such as a refund request.
  • Events you host: everything you put in a listing, including the venue name and street address (which may be your home), plus announcements, polls, tournaments and ticket types.

Payment information

  • If you buy a ticket, you enter your card details directly into Stripe Checkout. We don't receive or store card numbers. Stripe tells us whether a payment succeeded and gives us a payment reference, which we store with your registration along with the amount.
  • If you sell tickets, Stripe collects your identity, business and bank details during setup. We store only your Stripe account ID and whether your account can accept payments. Stripe handles that information under the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Information collected automatically

  • Cookies: two essential cookies, described in section 5.
  • IP address: used briefly in memory to limit repeated sign-in requests, and recorded in our web server and reverse proxy logs together with the page requested, the time and your browser type.
  • Emails we send you: we keep a copy of each email (address, subject, text and whether it was delivered) so failed messages can be retried and investigated.

We don't use analytics, advertising or social media trackers, and every page loads only from our own server: fonts and scripts included. We don't buy information about you from anyone else.

3. How we use it

  • To run {{ S }}: sign you in, show event pages, take registrations and payments, hold and assign seats, count votes and signups, and keep events from overselling.
  • To send you emails you need: sign-in links, registration confirmations, reminders a week and a day before events, cancellations and refund notices, and announcements from organizers of events you're registered for. We don't send marketing email.
  • To keep it safe: prevent abuse, fraud and spam, enforce our Terms, and investigate problems.
  • To meet legal obligations: for example keeping payment records and responding to lawful requests.

We don't sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running your account and registrations), our legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse and improving {{ S }}), and complying with legal obligations (payment records).

9. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Sign-in links are single-use and expire after 15 minutes, and we store them only in scrambled (hashed) form. Session cookies are signed so they can't be forged, and forms are protected against cross-site requests. We don't store passwords, because there aren't any. Access to the servers and database is limited to the people who run {{ S }}.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires. Please report security issues to {{ CONTACT }}.
